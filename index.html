APPROACH & PROCESS

A1
Texture First
Great retouching is invisible. Every frame is sculpted using non-destructive dodge-and-burn and micro-frequency separation so skin pores, flyaway hair, and fabric weave remain tactile and human.
P1
RAW & Lighting Audit
Evaluating 16-bit RAW captures, specular highlights, and shadow roll-off to establish a unified exposure baseline across the entire lookbook or campaign.
P2
Frequency Separation
Separating high-frequency pore structure from low-frequency tonal transitions to remove blemishes without plastic blur.
P3
Dodge & Burn Sculpting
Micro-level luminance contouring along cheekbones, collarbones, and garment folds to enhance three-dimensional depth.
P4
Editorial Color Grade
Crafting custom split-toning curves, true-to-life skin undertones, and filmic highlight warmth tailored to the brand identity.
P5
Glass & Product Polish
Rebuilding clean bottle geometry, liquid refraction, and label crispness for skincare, candle, and beverage key visuals.
P6
Typography & Grid
Pairing retouched imagery with editorial serif/grotesk typography for social carousels, lookbook spreads, and print brochures.
P7
Master Archive Delivery
Delivering color-calibrated CMYK print masters and sRGB digital assets ready for global campaign launch.
